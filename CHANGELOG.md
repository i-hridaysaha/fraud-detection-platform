# Changelog

All notable changes to this repository are recorded here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the versions follow
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html). Before 1.0.0 the repository was built
in ten stages, each merged and tagged `stage-NN`; those tags are the pre-release history and are
listed below under the version they roll up into.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- `scripts/case_study_numbers.py` and `tests/test_case_study_numbers.py`: every number the case
  study on the author's site quotes, printed from the artifacts in the article's spelling, with
  the script held to the artifacts in CI. ADR 0042 and `docs/notes/stage-11.md` carry the
  article's verification table (stage 11).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-13

The first release. Every number the README quotes is regenerated by `make reproduce` from the
two IEEE-CIS training files, on seed 42.

### Added

- README, METHODOLOGY.md, the architecture diagram, the ADR index, a MIT licence with the
  dataset excluded, this changelog, and `docs/verification.md` naming the artifact behind every
  number in the documentation (stage 10).
- `scripts/readme_numbers.py` and `tests/test_readme.py`: the README's tables are the script's
  output and the test fails when they and the artifacts disagree. `scripts/verify_numbers.py`
  matches every numeric token in the prose against an artifact leaf (stage 10).
- The lifecycle demo page, `docs/demo/index.html`, a static build of the stage 9 artifacts
  (stage 9 addendum, 2026-09-13).
- Monitoring and the lifecycle: a drift job with per-column PSI edges calibrated on the accepted
  windows, a decay job that reads only matured labels, adversarial validation, a promotion gate
  with a margin derived from the measured noise band, a challenger fit under a moved training
  boundary, registry promotion and rollback, and the replayed lifecycle demo (`stage-09`,
  2026-09-12).
- Serving: an online feature store (Redis or in-process) with a two-phase read-then-commit
  contract, a parity test that replays the first 30 days through the store against the batch
  build, the scoring service with calibration, cost bands and SHAP factors, MLflow registration
  by alias, the latency benchmark and the load test (`stage-08`, 2026-09-12).
- The experiments: the leakage delta (four shortcuts, one at a time and combined, against the
  causal baseline) and the simulated label latency (`stage-07`, 2026-09-12).
- Training and evaluation: three families by three imbalance strategies, block ablations over
  three seeds, a hyperparameter search on expanding-window folds, paired bootstrap intervals by
  row and by card, thresholds, cost bands, calibration, segments and SHAP (`stage-06`,
  2026-09-11).
- Graph features: an incremental union-find over six link columns with lagged labels, the
  finding that the graph is one component and its size a clock, and the measured transductive
  leak (`stage-05`, 2026-09-11).
- Point-in-time features: 36 features over three grains under a strict before-t rule, with
  brute-force and truncation-invariance tests (`stage-04`, 2026-09-11).
- Preparation: the column plan, transformations, the missing-value policy, frequency and lagged
  target encoding, the V-block reduction and the schema contract (`stage-03`, 2026-09-10).
- Exploratory analysis on the training split: target, univariate, missingness, correlation,
  bivariate, temporal, entity, duplicate and quality sections, each with an artifact and a
  figure (`stage-02`, 2026-09-10).
- The data layer: typed loading, the entity key, the chronological split and the train-only
  guard (`stage-01`, 2026-09-10).
- Scaffold, data acquisition script, the first-pass audit, tooling, CI and the decision-record
  format (`stage-00`, 2026-09-10).

### Changed

- The test suite runs on push rather than on every commit (between `stage-05` and `stage-06`,
  2026-09-11).
